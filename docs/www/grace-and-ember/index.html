Welcome to Grace & Ember – Hosted By BK Stays

Wake up to year-round ridge views & breathtaking sunrises. Spend the day exploring Blue Ridge, and return to your secluded mountain retreat with no direct neighbors & peaceful mountain scenery. Soak in the jacuzzi beneath the stars, unwind with cocktails in the speakeasy, or recharge in the private home gym before doing it all again.
