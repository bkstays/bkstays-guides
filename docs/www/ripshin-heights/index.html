Welcome to Ripshin Heights!

– Hosted By BK Stays

You won't find more incredible Blue Ridge mountain or lake views than right here. This luxury modern retreat, with 4 bedrooms and 4 bathrooms, sleeps 8 and is only 10 min from downtown! Designed with high-end finishes, 3 king suites, multiple fireplaces, and a large jacuzzi, this executive escape offers rare panoramic views and refined comfort for upscale adult group getaways. Indulge in the elevated mountain escape you and your friends deserve!

Main Level Living Room

The heart of the home, designed for elevated stays, relaxed evenings, and unforgettable views

  • Open-concept great room with soaring ceilings, stone gas fireplace, and 60" Smart TV
  • Oversized leather seating with warm textures and modern mountain design
  • Expansive windows and sliding doors showcasing panoramic mountain + lake views
  • Seamless indoor-outdoor flow to covered deck with fireplace and lounge seating
  • Designed for upscale entertaining with direct connection to kitchen and dining

Downstairs Media Room

An ideal secondary gathering space for elevated movie nights, cocktails, games, and effortless entertaining.

  • 60" Smart TV and Gas Stone Fireplace
  • Leather Seating with Ample Lounge Space
  • Kitchenette, Wine Cooler, Barware and Glassware
  • Air Hockey and Game/Card Table + Record Player
  • Walkout Patio and More Beautiful Mountain Views

Bedrooms

Each bedroom is thoughtfully designed with large Smart TVs, luxury linens, extra pillows and blankets, ample clothing storage, and breathtaking Blue Ridge mountain and lake views.

  • 1st Bed (Master on Main) – King bed, outdoor patio access with panoramic mountain + lake views, vaulted ceilings and designer lighting
  • 2nd Bed (Main Level) - Full + Twin, patio access, vaulted ceilings, comfortable workspace & seating area with direct access to Bath 2 (ensuite optional)
  • 3rd Bed (Lower Level) - King bed with private ensuite bath; a peaceful retreat with mountain views
  • 4th Bed (Lower Level) - Queen bed, adjacent to bathroom 4, with a dedicated workspace, warm wood finishes with mountain views

Kitchen & Dining

Designed for gathering, entertaining, and savoring the best of your Blue Ridge mountain getaway, whether dining in or exploring downtown minutes away.

  • Open-concept kitchen overlooking the dining area
  • Oversized waterfall island with bar seating for 4
  • Five-burner gas range and double ovens
  • Stainless steel appliance package + wine fridge
  • Farmhouse sink and house water filtration system
  • Dining table with seating for 8 (bench + chairs)
  • Fully stocked cookware, bakeware and flatware
  • KitchenAid, blender, crockpot, processor + toaster
  • Glassware for wine, bourbon, cocktail + drinking
  • 3-burner propane grill with grill utensils
  • Coffee Bar includes a Keurig and regular drip coffee maker, with grounds, K-cups, filters, creamer, sugars, and full coffee essentials provided

Bathrooms

4 Total Bathrooms (Including 3 Private En-suites)

All bathrooms have plush towels, hair dryers, and premium bath essentials, including shampoo, conditioner, body wash, and lotion.

  • 1st Bath (Master) - Heated floors, dual vanity with double sinks, large glass walk-in shower with rainfall + second shower head, and a standalone copper soaking tub
  • 2nd Bath (2nd ensuite) - Floor-to-ceiling stone walk-in shower, pebble flooring and glass enclosure, live-edge wood vanity, stone vessel sink, designer finishes, and rustic-modern details
  • 3rd Bath (3rd ensuite) - Dual vanity, double sinks, quartz countertops, tub/shower combo, custom tile, warm wood cabinetry, and refined mountain-modern finishes
  • 4th Bath - Glass walk-in shower, sleek black tile, geometric flooring, warm wood vanity, and modern brass fixtures; a stylish, design-forward space
  • A full-size washer and dryer with detergent, along with an ironing board and iron, are provided for added convenience
