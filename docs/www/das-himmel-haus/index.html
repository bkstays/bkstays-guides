Welcome to Das Himmel Haus - Hosted By BK Stays

5 King Bedrooms, 3 Bathrooms, and Amazing Views!

This spacious family-friendly cabin (sleeps 12) is located inside Shanty Creek Resort, only minutes from Schuss Mountain, and includes year-round access to indoor and outdoor pools.

Exterior amenities include a relaxing jacuzzi, cedar sauna, and cozy firepit area, while indoors offers a gas fireplace, spacious living room, and open-concept kitchen and dining perfect for gathering and entertaining.

  • Barrel Sauna (4-person cedar sauna)
  • Outdoor fire pit area (S'mores kit included)
  • Spacious private deck w/ outdoor dining space

Outdoor Fun & Relaxation

Designed for relaxation and connection, this outdoor retreat invites guests to unwind in the jacuzzi, rejuvenate in the cedar sauna, and take in stunning mountain views after a full day of golfing, boating, or skiing nearby.

  • Fire Pit w/ Adirondack, bench seating + lighting
  • 7-person covered Jacuzzi with stunning views
  • 4-person cedar-barrel sauna above the valley
  • Spacious deck w/ panoramic mountain views
  • Rocking chairs for morning or sunset relaxation
  • Outdoor dining space w/ seating for large groups
  • Private and relatively secluded wooded setting
