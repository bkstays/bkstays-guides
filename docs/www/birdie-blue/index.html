The fully equipped kitchen has sleek stainless steel appliances and everything needed to prepare a holiday feast. Just steps away, the dining area offers the ideal setting for meals, entertaining, and conversation, making every moment feel special.

Head upstairs to the loft, a fun-filled retreat perfect for kids and adults. Enjoy three newly installed arcade machines, a variety of board games, puzzles, and card games, plus an extra TV for all your gaming needs. It's the ultimate space for entertainment and making memories!
