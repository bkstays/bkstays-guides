About us

Meet Brandon, Katharina, Emmy & Leo

We're the family behind BK Stays. We host every home the way we'd want to be hosted: spotless, thoughtfully stocked and with a real person a message away.

Our story

Modern Comfort. Scenic Retreats. Hosts Who Care.

We met in Atlanta more than 12 years ago, got married at a destination wedding in Mexico, and have been chasing adventures together ever since. Today we're a family of four, plus three rescue dogs, and life is a happy blur of Emmy's activities, Leo's activities and weekends away whenever we can get them.

BK Stays started in year with our first home. What began as one cabin has grown to 14 homes across Georgia, Florida, Michigan and Minnesota, and we still care for every one of them like it's our own getaway.

Brandon grew up in Iowa, played college football and has spent his career in sports, current role. Katharina is a dual citizen, born and raised in Germany, and current role.
